About Us

Backed by extensive telecommunications and IoT expertise, TechMonton delivers enterprise-grade hardware integration, real-time remote monitoring, and automated control for fleets, agriculture, and smart properties.

1. Fleet Telematics & Vehicle Security

Complete remote monitoring and anti-theft management for cars, commercial trucks, and passenger buses.

  • Hardware Integration: Expert setup for Teltonika, GoSafe, and JimiLab devices (OBD & hardwired).
  • Live Tracking & Security: Real-time GPS location, geofencing, remote engine kill/cut-off, and fuel monitoring.
  • Cabin & Road View: Dual dashcams & MDVR integration for live video streaming inside and outside vehicles.

2. Smart Agriculture & Livestock Automation

Modernizing farm management with reliable IoT telemetry and off-grid surveillance.

  • Livestock Monitoring: Installation of smart cow collars/tags to track vitals, movement, rumination, and estrus cycles.
  • Specialized Farms: Customized remote monitoring solutions for cattle, ostrich, and poultry operations.
  • Remote Security: Solar-powered surveillance cameras for off-grid perimeter defense and continuous site monitoring.

3. Smart Home & Energy Automation

Intelligent control systems to monitor energy use and secure living spaces from anywhere.

  • Appliance & Power Control: Installation of smart plugs, switches, and relays for automated scheduling and remote power cut-off.
  • Integrated Security: HD indoor/outdoor IP cameras with motion detection and mobile alerts.
